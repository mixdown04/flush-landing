Your data is yours

Built with privacy at the foundation,
not bolted on after the fact.

A lot of people ask if we can see their financial data. The honest answer is no, and here is why that is true by design.

🏦
Bank connections via Plaid
Your bank credentials never touch Flush. Plaid, the same infrastructure used by Venmo, Coinbase, and most major fintech apps, handles authentication and passes back read-only transaction data.
🔒
Your data is invisible to everyone else
Every query in the database is scoped to your account using Row Level Security. Not even the Flush team can run a query that returns your data. Your numbers are yours only.
💳
Payments via Stripe
Card details never touch Flush servers. All payment processing runs through Stripe, which is PCI Level 1 certified and used by millions of businesses worldwide.

Read the full details on our Security page and Privacy Policy.
