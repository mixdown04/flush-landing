Flush vs Harvest

Harvest tracks your hours.
Flush tracks your money.

Harvest is genuinely excellent time tracking software for agencies and teams billing by the hour. But if you're a solo founder or consultant who needs to know where your cash is going, what's coming in next quarter, and how much to set aside for taxes — Harvest stops where Flush starts.

Bottom line
Choose Flush if…
You're a solo founder or consultant who doesn't bill primarily by the hour.
You need to know what your cash position will look like in 30, 60, or 90 days.
You want automatic tax set-aside recommendations after every client payment.
You hold equity alongside your consulting work and want it all tracked in one place.
You want a flat $29/month rate that doesn't grow as you add clients.
Choose Harvest if…
You run an agency or team and need to track billable hours across multiple people and projects.
Project profitability — hours billed vs budget — is your primary financial metric.
You need timesheet approvals, team utilization reporting, and per-project budget alerts.
